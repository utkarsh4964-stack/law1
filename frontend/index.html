WORKFLOW

A case file with a clear chain of evidence.

LAW1 connects the investigation from the first upload through verification and reporting.

01

Secure the evidence

Upload documents, validate them and record their SHA-256 hash within the case chain of custody.

02

Structure the file

Extract relevant entities, events and claims from the evidence so the investigation can be reviewed systematically.

03

Connect the evidence

Follow chronology, relationships and conflicting claims across the documents in the case.

04

Prepare the record

Generate a structured case report while retaining the underlying evidence and audit history.
